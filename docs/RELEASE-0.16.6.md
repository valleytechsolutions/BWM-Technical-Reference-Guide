# Black Wire 0.16.6

First Edition / 2026 · Digital collection 2026.11.3

This desktop release carries 24 physical pinout references across 22 Tindie listings. Seventeen listings add missing boards or carrier variants. Seven drawings fill gaps for the GLYPH C3, C6 and H2, the HiiBot IoTs2 and the RP2040 PicoADK. [Full model and source register](https://github.com/valleytechsolutions/black-wire-pinouts/blob/v2026.11.3/docs/EXPANSION-2026.11.3.md)

Four originals are available in the offline library. These cover the Achiraware ESP32-C3 and RP2350A boards, the rectangular Solder Party RP2040 Stamp Carrier and the Datanoise PicoADK header template. The other 20 drawings open at their original URLs. The Tall Dog carrier records identify the relevant page in each seller PDF.

Check the revision before wiring the HiiBot IoTs2. The V1 drawing assigns SPI clock to GPIO40 and the button to GPIO21. V2 assigns them to GPIO39 and GPIO0. Two other seller diagrams remain on hold for a USB signal discrepancy on the Achiraware ESP32-S3 and a ground-pad numbering discrepancy on the RP2350B BellsNWhistles.

In **Updates & offline library**, choose **Check for updates**. Install the update and choose **Download offline library** to save the four new originals.
