## HiiBot IoTs2 V1 and V2

Match the marking on the PCB. SPI clock moves from GPIO40 on V1 to GPIO39 on V2. The user button moves from GPIO21 to GPIO0.

[Open the HiiBot IoTs2 record](https://valleytech-black-wire-guide.pages.dev/?board=hangzhou-leban-esp32-hiibot-iots2)

## Tall Dog carrier revisions

Select the Teensy host version and the carrier printed on your board. The Standard, DIP-64, Uno and Due carriers for Teensy 3.5/3.6 have distinct header layouts. Each entry points to the seller PDF and identifies its pinout page.



## Drawings on hold

The Achiraware ESP32-S3 map conflicts with the seller schematic on native USB D− and D+. The RP2350B BellsNWhistles map labels J8 ground as pin 6, while the maker schematic and PCB place it on pin 8. Both drawings remain on hold.
