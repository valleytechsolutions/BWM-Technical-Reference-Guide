## Match the revision printed on the board

Look for v1.2 on the back of the Seeed XIAO nRF54L15 or Sense before using the rear pad map. The 3V3, RST2, SWCLK2 and SWDIO2 pads moved. Both V1.2 models use the same rear drawing. On Arduino Modulino Thermo, match the PCB marking and sensor to the HS3003 or GXHT30C diagram.

[XIAO nRF54L15 Sense V1.2](https://valleytech-black-wire-guide.pages.dev/?board=seeed-studio-xiao-nrf54l15-sense-v1-2) · [Arduino Nano XL Link pinouts](https://valleytech-black-wire-guide.pages.dev/?board=arduino-nano-xl-link)

Search for T-Display CM0, T-CAN485 C5, LP-EM-CC2341R10 or Waveshare ESP32-S31-Touch-LCD-4.3C. Open Online diagrams for the manufacturer’s original.
