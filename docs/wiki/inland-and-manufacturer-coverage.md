# Inland and manufacturer coverage

Match Inland SKUs and DFRobot variants, inspect the original diagrams and see what remains missing.

## Find Inland by model or SKU

Search Inland, Pro Micro, UNO Plus, ESP32, RC522 or the six-digit Micro Center SKU. The current sweep records 67 Inland boards, modules, shields, sensors and kits from 48 support articles, 49 retail listings and an additional archived kit guide. Eight physical pinout images are saved. The remaining records distinguish connector labels, wiring examples, photographs and missing sheets.



## Similar names can hide different hardware

The USB-C ESP32 and 2.8-inch display are separate from older ESP32 and generic CYD boards. Current PIR and breadboard power-module photos differ from the old support articles linked by the retailer. Match the pictured PCB before using a reference. The ESP-01 source contains a reset-label conflict; it is not a validated wiring instruction.



## DFRobot models and original documents

The 50 official MCU-category SKU pages include RP2040, RP2350, C5, C5-U, C6, S3, P4, AVR and other families. Component datasheets remain separate from board manuals and schematics. DFR1139 has conflicting N16R8/N16R2 labels in the manufacturer page. The broader 807-product wiki inventory is a queue for further research.



## Inspect coverage rather than assume completion

Distributor leads are attributed to the actual hardware manufacturer. TI LaunchPad, Microchip AVR-IoT WG and Silicon Labs references include their source PDF pages. An available document does not prove every pin is covered. No worldwide or entire-brand completeness is claimed.
