## Read the connector and its purpose

Selected XIAO edge contacts and M5Stack HY2.0, Hat2-Bus and Cardputer-Adv EXT connectors have searchable source-backed pin-purpose tables. They cover 159 external contacts; internal GPIO allocation tables are not presented as exposed terminals. Use the matching manufacturer image for physical orientation and check all voltage and configuration requirements.



## Keep reference types distinct

The 232 added references include 14 physical pinout images, 152 original PDFs, 13 pin-function sheets and 53 supporting label, GPIO or connector references. Arduino carrier pages distinguish 3.3 V MCU signals from 1.8 V MPU signals. Renesas manual pages retain jumper, switch and unpopulated-header notes. Source photos, block diagrams and schematics do not establish a complete physical pinout.
