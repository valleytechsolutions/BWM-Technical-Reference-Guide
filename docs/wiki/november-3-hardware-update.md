# November 3: new pinouts, sensors and hardware documentation

Collection 2026.11.1 adds 15 listings, updates three existing listings and saves 33 references, including nine physical pinout images. Includes the November 7 Cerberus addition.

## November 7: Cerberus by 463n7.io

The supplied Cerberus hardware pinout is now searchable by Cerberus, 463n7.io, ESP32-S3 and ESP32-C5, with a Devices & IoT listing. The original 1638 × 1091 image maps the physical J1/J2 headers, component side with antennas up. Manufacturer specifications are linked separately; the supplied artwork has no identified PCB revision or independently established diagram license. Alternate-function badges and shared onboard signals do not establish unused peripheral pins.

[Open Cerberus in the guide](https://valleytech-black-wire-guide.pages.dev/?board=463n7-io-cerberus) · [463n7.io manufacturer specifications](https://463n7.io/products/cerberus-development-board)

## Physical board pinouts

Waveshare ESP32-S31-Zero, Wireless-Tag WT9932P4C61-TINY and WT9932P4-TINY, Unexpected Maker TinyC6[D] and TinyPICO[D], and Sipeed Tang Nano and Tang Nano 9K now have original physical pinout images. The existing TinyPICO Nano listing gained a clearly V4-scoped pad map. Revision notes, creator attribution and source links accompany each reference. Images are source references, not independent approval of every electrical limit or connector.



## Sensors, NFC and expansion boards

SparkFun BMI330 and BMA456 breakouts, Adafruit RV-8803, and ELECHOUSE ST25R3916B Mini V2 have supporting labels and hardware documentation. Mini V2 ships in I2C mode; its manual includes older V1 current measurements that must not be treated as V2 tested limits. Milk-V Titan V2.1, Antmicro Jetson Orin PCIe expansion and Electronic Cats CAN bus add-on references retain their supporting-only scope. Schematics, port labels and cable diagrams are not counted as complete board pinouts.



## New kits and documented gaps

The November 3 Seeed OpenDisplay 4.26-inch Mono and 7.3-inch Color kit names now find their existing EN05 and EN04 board records. The source pass attempted all 163 existing brand labels and 256 seed URLs, with 49 initial retrieval failures. Selected follow-ups found both fresh documentation and older omissions; this is not an exhaustive inventory. Heltec RadioCore rights/source conflicts, Orange Pi 6 Plus retrieval failures and the Si5351C/older-Si5351A guide mismatch remain explicit research gaps.

[Detailed findings, dates and source register](https://github.com/valleytechsolutions/black-wire-pinouts/blob/main/docs/EXPANSION-2026.11.1.md)

## Updating your desktop library

Collection 2026.11.1 is bundled with app 0.16.4. Check the release page for validated installers. In a supported installed app, open Updates & offline library, choose Check for updates, Download update, then Restart & update. After restart, Download offline library saves the new references and reuses unchanged verified files. Windows and Linux package validation is reported with the release; no macOS binary or publisher signing is implied.
