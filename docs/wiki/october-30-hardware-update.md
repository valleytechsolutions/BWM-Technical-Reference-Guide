# October 30: new hardware references and desktop collection update

Collection 2026.10.6 adds 13 listings, updates one board and saves 39 references.

## New physical pinouts

Wireless-Tag WT9932S31-TINY, Adafruit Metro ESP32-P4 Hydra, Waveshare ESP32-S3-LR1121-ePaper-2.9, Waveshare ESP32-P4-Core and M5Stack Stamp Timer Power2 have original physical pinout references. Manufacturer manuals, schematics and exact model/revision notes accompany the maps. ESP32-S31 remains distinct from ESP32-S3 in search.



## Sensors and single-board computers

M5Stack Unit DoF6, DoF9, DoF10 and BMM350 were released on October 23 according to the manufacturer. Manuals, connector labels and component datasheet links are available. LattePanda Sigma 2 has ten connector/switch diagrams and manufacturer specifications. Supporting connector labels and diagrams are not counted as complete physical board maps.



## Earlier omissions and open gaps

Wireless-Tag WTDKP4C5-S1, WT99P461-S1 and WT9932P4-MINI-A1 manuals and schematics are now saved. The existing Waveshare ESP32-DEV-KIT-XX gained its schematic without duplicating an unchanged pinout. Source discovery reached all 163 existing brand labels; inaccessible sources and missing physical maps remain explicit. This is not a complete worldwide inventory.
