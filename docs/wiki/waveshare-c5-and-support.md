# C5 boards, new references and project support

Waveshare C5 pinouts, M5Stack and NodeMCU reference updates, and the optional Patreon link.

## Find your C5 board

ESP32-C5-Pico and ESP32-C5-MINI-KIT each already had two pinout images. Their current manufacturer pinout images match the saved hashes. Original board schematics and M/U/UM variant aliases are now included. Search ESP32-C5 Mini or the full model name, then match the exact PCB revision.

## New and expanded references

Collection 2026.10.3 adds Waveshare ESP32-S3-LR1121-OLED-1.54, ESP32-S3-RS485-WLED and the older M5Stack Base PLC-M12 v1.1 revision. ToughC5, Unit 8Servos2-I2C and NodeMCU DevKit 0.9/1.0 gain references. Eleven attachments include two partial physical pinouts, six PDFs, two label images and a function sheet. WLED terminal functions distinguish 5 V level-shifted LED outputs from 3.3 V GPIOs.



## Coverage stays explicit

All nine products in the current Waveshare C5 documentation category are tracked; complete pinout approval is separate. M5Stack releases through the latest listed October 9 entries were checked. No new official NodeMCU hardware release was established. Failed downloads and unresolved revision/alias leads remain recorded. Source artwork retains its own rights. First Edition / 2026 and the book draft are unchanged.
