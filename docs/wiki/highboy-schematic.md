## Match the hardware revision

V2_03 comes from the supplied filename; the drawing revision boxes are blank. Original sheet totals are inconsistent, and prototype annotations remain present. Final production status and electrical correctness have not been independently verified. The historical REV 2.2 MVP uses ESP32-S3 and has a separate connector-label image. Do not transfer pin assignments between the two revisions.
